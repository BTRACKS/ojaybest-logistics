Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective date: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information Ojaybest Logistics collects, why we need it, who receives it and how you can manage it. It applies to this website and to the orders, enquiries and shipments we handle for you.

1. Who we are

Ojaybest Logistics is a logistics business registered and operating from Port Harcourt, Nigeria, providing international shipping, freight forwarding and warehousing services to businesses and individuals. We are the company responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

  • Registered business name: Ojaybest Logistics.
  • Address: Port Harcourt, Nigeria.
  • Email for privacy enquiries: hello@ojaybestlogistics.com.
  • Phone: +234 800 000 0000.
  • Effective date of this policy: 24 October 2026.

2. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to quote, book and deliver your shipments:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses, and recipient names, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Shipment information: item descriptions, quantities, weights, dimensions and declared values.
  • Messages you send us through our forms, by email, on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Tracking codes you enter on our Track Order pages.
  • Your email address if you subscribe to our updates.

We do not currently collect payment card details on this website. Where payment is arranged by bank transfer or invoice, we hold only the records needed to confirm and reconcile your payment. We do not collect account registrations at this time.

3. How we collect information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • When you complete the Create Order form on this website.
  • When you complete our Contact Us form.
  • When you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp to enquire or book.
  • When you enter a tracking code on our Track Order pages.
  • When you subscribe to updates in the newsletter box on our website.

Our website does not currently use advertising or analytics tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy before the tools go live.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Prepare quotes and process your bookings.
  • Arrange collection, shipping, customs clearance and delivery of your shipments.
  • Give you tracking updates and let you follow shipments on our website.
  • Respond to your enquiries and handle complaints and claims.
  • Manage payments, invoices and refunds for your orders.
  • Prevent misuse of our services and meet our legal obligations.
  • Send you updates, where you have asked to receive them.

5. Grounds for using information

We rely on different bases depending on what we are doing with your information:

  • Performing our contract with you when we quote, book, move and deliver your shipments and handle claims and refunds.
  • Meeting our legal obligations for customs, tax, accounting and record-keeping.
  • Your consent for optional marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Our legitimate interests in running and protecting our business, such as keeping records of enquiries and preventing misuse, in ways you would reasonably expect.

6. Information we share

We share the information needed to move your shipment, and only what each recipient needs to do their part:

  • Delivery partners and last-mile couriers, who need names, addresses and phone numbers to deliver.
  • Freight carriers (airlines and shipping lines) and their agents, who need shipment and consignee details to accept and release cargo.
  • Warehouse operators, who need collection and storage details to handle your goods.
  • Customs authorities and their agents, who legally require consignee, contents and value information to clear shipments.
  • Our website and email service providers, who store or process form submissions and messages on our behalf.
  • Professional advisers, insurers or authorities, where needed to handle a claim, dispute or legal requirement.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

7. International data transfers

Because we arrange international shipments, recipient and shipment details are necessarily shared with carriers, customs authorities and delivery partners outside Nigeria. That sharing is what makes international delivery possible, and it is limited to the information the shipment requires.

Where information is stored with a service provider outside Nigeria, we choose providers that offer safeguards for information transferred internationally, such as contractual commitments to protect it to Nigerian data-protection standards. We will update this policy if the way we host or transfer information changes.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our website currently uses only the basic functionality a website needs to work, for example, remembering your preferences as you move between pages. We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools that track you across sites.

You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential functionality may stop some parts of the site from working. If we introduce optional cookies or analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and explain them here.

9. Data storage and security

We keep customer information on secure systems, limit access to the team members who need it to handle your bookings, and share it outside our business only as described in section 6. Messages and form submissions are stored on the systems of the services we use to receive them.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. If something ever goes wrong with information we hold, we will investigate, contain it and tell the people affected and the regulator where the law requires it.

10. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it:

  • Order and shipment records: for 6 years after the shipment is completed, so we can handle claims, refunds, tax and accounting checks.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: for 12 months, so we can pick up the conversation if you come back.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.

Where Nigerian tax or customs law requires us to keep records longer, we keep them for that period. After that, we delete the information or remove anything that identifies you.

11. Customer privacy rights

You can ask us to do the following with the information we hold about you, by emailing hello@ojaybestlogistics.com:

  • Tell you what information we hold and give you a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete information we no longer need to keep.
  • Stop using your information for marketing.
  • Explain how we have used your information or raise a concern about it.

We respond to requests within 30 days. Some information (for example, customs declarations or tax records) must be kept by law even if you ask for deletion. We never charge for a request, and we may ask for proof of identity before acting on one, so we do not release information to the wrong person.

12. Marketing communications

If you subscribe in the newsletter box on our website, we send occasional updates about our services, routes and offers. That is the only marketing we do by email.

Shipment updates are different: if you have an order with us, we will contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your booking, tracking and delivery because we need to complete the service. That is not marketing. You can stop marketing emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by telling us at hello@ojaybestlogistics.com.

13. Children's information

Our services are for businesses and adults arranging shipments. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and our website is not directed at children.

In ordinary deliveries, a child's name may appear as a recipient on a parcel the same way any recipient's would; we handle that information only to deliver the shipment. If you believe a child has given us information they should not have, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Contact and complaints

For anything about your privacy, contact us at hello@ojaybestlogistics.com, on +234 800 000 0000, or through our Contact Us page. Tell us what you would like us to do and include any booking or tracking details.

We will acknowledge your message within 2 working days and respond with what we have done within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, the regulator for data protection in Nigeria, through its official channels.

15. Policy updates

This policy takes effect on 24 October 2026. We may update it as our services, systems or legal duties change; the current version is always published on this page with its effective date.

When a change is significant (for example, a new way we collect or share information), we will announce it on our website and, where we have your contact details, tell you by email or WhatsApp before it takes effect.
