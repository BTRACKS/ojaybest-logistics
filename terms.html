2. Services covered

These terms apply to the services you confirm with us, which may include international shipping (air and sea freight), freight forwarding, warehousing solutions, and last-mile delivery for parcels, cargo and inventory.

Where part of a journey is handled by a third-party carrier, airline, shipping line, customs agent or warehouse partner, we arrange that leg on your behalf. Those providers apply their own operating rules, and we remain your single point of contact for the service you booked.

3. Orders and bookings

You can place an order through our website, by phone, by email or on WhatsApp. A booking becomes confirmed once we have your shipment details and written or spoken confirmation of the quoted price, and payment has been made or agreed for the leg of the journey being booked.

To request a change to a confirmed booking, contact us as early as possible. We will always try to accommodate changes, but some changes (for example, after collection or after documents have been lodged with a carrier) may attract additional costs charged at cost.

4. Customer responsibilities

You agree to provide, and are responsible for, the following:

  • Accurate sender and recipient names, phone numbers and delivery addresses.
  • Honest and complete descriptions of every item, including quantities and declared values.
  • Any documents required for your shipment, such as invoices, permits or identification needed for customs.
  • Being reachable on the contact details you gave us while your shipment is in transit.

We rely on the information you provide when quoting, routing and clearing your shipment. We are not responsible for delays, extra charges or losses caused by inaccurate, incomplete or false information.

5. Packaging

Unless we agree otherwise in writing, packaging is the customer's responsibility, and goods must be packed to survive the journey by road, air or sea. We can advise on, or arrange, suitable packaging on request.

Fragile, perishable and high-value goods must be declared before booking so that we can agree suitable protective packaging and handling arrangements in advance. Undeclared fragile or valuable items travel at the owner's risk to the extent our standard packaging and handling cannot reasonably protect them.

6. Prohibited and restricted items

We do not accept shipments containing:

  • Illegal or controlled substances, counterfeit goods, or weapons and ammunition.
  • Explosives, flammables and other dangerous goods that are not properly declared and documented.
  • Live animals, human remains, and currency or negotiable instruments where prohibited by the carrier.
  • Any item whose carriage would break Nigerian law or the law of any country on the route.

Some items, such as electronics, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, foodstuffs and high-value goods, are restricted: they require advance approval and special documentation. Always check with us before booking; if a prohibited item is found, the shipment may be stopped, returned or reported to the authorities, and any resulting costs are the customer's responsibility.

7. Pricing and payment

Charges are based on the service, route, weight or volume (whichever applies, including dimensional weight), declared value, and any extras you request such as packaging, storage or insurance. Quotes are given before booking and are valid for the period stated on the quote (usually 7 days) unless fuel, exchange-rate or carrier price changes apply in the meantime.

Payment is due by the deadline shown on your invoice, and in most cases before dispatch. We may hold a shipment until payment clears. If a third party (for example an airline, customs agent or storage provider) charges us extra after booking, we will explain the reason and pass the cost on at cost. We do not add hidden fees.

8. Customs and duties

The customer is responsible for supplying accurate customs documents (commercial invoices, packing lists, permits and any other paperwork the destination requires) and for paying import duties, taxes and clearance charges. We can prepare and lodge documents on your behalf as part of our freight forwarding service.

Delays, inspections, seizures or extra charges caused by incomplete documents, undervaluation or prohibited contents are the customer's responsibility. Where a shipment is inspected by customs, any inspection or demurrage costs that apply are passed on at cost.

9. Delivery and tracking

We give estimated delivery times when you book, and we work hard to keep them. Estimates are not guarantees: weather, customs, carrier schedules, strikes and other events outside our control can cause delays. When a delay happens, we will tell you as soon as we know and keep you updated.

You can follow your shipment with your tracking code on our Track Order page, and our team can give updates by phone, WhatsApp or email. A delivery is confirmed when the recipient (or an authorised person at the address) signs for the package, or when we record proof of delivery such as a photo or digital acknowledgement.

10. Failed delivery and storage

If the recipient is unavailable or refuses delivery, we will attempt contact using the details provided and hold the shipment for collection or redelivery. If an address is incorrect or incomplete and the shipment cannot be delivered, we will contact you to confirm the right address before attempting again.

Goods left uncollected after we have made reasonable attempts to reach you may attract reasonable storage charges after the first 7 days, and after 30 days without arrangement we may return the goods or dispose of them, with any unpaid charges deducted from the value of the goods. We will always try to agree a plan with you before taking either step.

11. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel a booking at any time by contacting us. Where nothing has been spent on your behalf (no collection, no documents lodged, no space booked with a carrier), you receive a full refund. Once work has started, we deduct the actual costs already incurred (collection, documentation, carrier fees, storage) and refund the balance.

Refund requests are handled through our customer channels, and refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 working days of the cancellation being agreed. This does not affect any cancellation or refund rights you have under Nigerian consumer law.

12. Loss, damage and claims

If a shipment is lost or arrives damaged, tell us as soon as you discover it. Please report the problem within 7 days of delivery (or within 21 days if the shipment is missing), and support your claim with:

  • Your tracking code and booking details.
  • Photos of the packaging and the goods.
  • The invoice, receipt or other evidence of the items' value.

We investigate every claim and assess compensation against the declared value of the affected items and the cover that applies to your booking. Claims made after the reporting window, or for items not declared at booking, may not be payable. We will explain our findings and any settlement in writing.

13. Insurance and liability

Every shipment includes our standard care and handling. Additional insurance against loss or damage is available on request. Tell us the declared value at booking and we will quote the premium. Insurance does not cover inadequate packaging, prohibited items, or consequential losses such as lost profits.

Our responsibility for any shipment is limited to the value of the goods as declared (or, where no value was declared, a reasonable amount reflecting the service paid for), and does not cover delays or losses caused by events outside our reasonable control, by inaccurate customer information, or by customs decisions. These limits apply to the fullest extent permitted by law and do not reduce any rights you have that cannot lawfully be limited.

14. Privacy

We collect and use your information only to quote, book, move and track your shipments, and to keep you informed. How we handle customer information is described in full in our separate Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms.

15. Complaints and disputes

If something goes wrong, contact us first by email at hello@ojaybestlogistics.com, by phone on +234 800 000 0000, on WhatsApp, or through our Contact Us page. Tell us what happened and include your tracking code.

We acknowledge complaints within 2 working days, investigate, and give you our findings and proposed remedy in writing, normally within 10 working days. If we cannot resolve a dispute between us, Nigerian law applies to these terms and either party may take the matter to the courts or consumer-protection bodies of Nigeria.

16. Changes to the terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The current version is always published on this page, and we will announce significant changes on our website and, where we have your contact details, by email or WhatsApp.

A booking is governed by the version of these terms in force on the date the booking was confirmed, unless you and we agree in writing that a newer version applies.

17. Contact

Questions about these terms, a booking, or a claim can be sent any time to hello@ojaybestlogistics.com or +234 800 000 0000, or through the form on our Contact Us page. We are happy to walk through anything here in plain language before you book.
