Frequently asked questions

Practical answers to the questions customers ask before booking and while waiting for their shipment.

We handle local and international shipping, air and sea freight forwarding, warehousing and last-mile delivery for individuals and businesses, end to end.

We deliver across Nigeria and ship by air and sea to over 200 international destinations, handling export documentation and customs clearance for you.

Click Create Order, fill in the pickup and delivery details, describe your package and confirm. You will receive a tracking number immediately.

No account is needed. You only provide your contact details, the recipient's details, the pickup and delivery addresses, and a description of your shipment.

Pricing depends on the weight, size, destination and speed of your shipment. Request a quote through Create Order or Contact Us for a tailored rate.

We accept bank transfers, debit cards, and pay-on-pickup for local deliveries. Business accounts can request monthly invoicing.

You can ship most everyday goods. Dangerous goods, flammables, explosives, narcotics and cash are not accepted. The full list is in our Terms & Conditions.

Domestic deliveries typically arrive in 1–3 business days. International air freight takes about 5–10 business days, while sea freight takes 4–8 weeks depending on the destination.

Enter your OJBTR tracking number on the Track Order page to see every checkpoint of your shipment, from pickup to delivery.

Statuses update at each checkpoint, so allow a few hours between scans. If nothing has changed for more than 24 hours, please Contact Us with your tracking number.

Contact Us with your tracking number as soon as you notice a problem. Our team will investigate and, where a claim applies, guide you through the process under our Terms & Conditions.

Call +234 800 000 0000, email hello@ojaybestlogistics.com or send a message through the Contact Us page. We reply quickly during business hours.
